📲 How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android and save the file.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Allow Install unknown apps if asked.
  3. If Play Protect says "App blocked", see the fix below.
  4. Open MyBubble, type your number, and tap Save number.
  5. Tap Start teddy and allow Display over other apps.
  6. Tap Accessibility settings, open MyBubble, and turn it On.
  7. Tap a text box in any app, then tap the teddy. Done! 🎉

🛠️ Problems and fixes

🚫 "App blocked to protect your device" (Play Protect)

Play Protect treats Accessibility apps as sensitive. MyBubble only uses it to type your number.

  1. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Play Protect.
  2. Tap the gear icon and turn off Scan apps with Play Protect.
  3. Install the app again.
  4. Turn Play Protect back on.
❌ "App not installed"

Uninstall any older MyBubble first, then install again. Also check you have free storage.

🔒 Accessibility switch is greyed out ("Restricted setting")
  1. Phone Settings, then Apps, then MyBubble.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Allow restricted settings and confirm.
  4. Go to Accessibility and turn MyBubble on.

If you don't see ⋮, open Accessibility and tap MyBubble once, then go back to the App info page.

👻 The teddy does not appear

Tap Start teddy in the app. Allow Display over other apps and notifications. Make sure the mode is not set to Hidden. If it is set to "Only with a text box", tap a text box first.

⌨️ The number does not get typed

Check that you saved a number and that MyBubble is on in Accessibility. Tap a text box so the keyboard appears before tapping the teddy. After updating the app, turn Accessibility off and on once. Some apps, like banking apps and password fields, block this on purpose.

😴 The teddy vanishes or slides off the edge

That is the edge dock. It tucks half off-screen after 2.5 seconds. Touch the visible half to bring it back.

🔋 The teddy disappears after a while

Your phone's battery saver may be stopping it. Go to Settings, Apps, MyBubble, Battery, and choose Unrestricted. On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, or Realme, also enable Autostart for MyBubble.

🔇 No sound

Turn the Sounds switch on in the app. Sounds use your media volume, so turn it up.

🎥 I am recording my screen

The teddy shows up in recordings. Pull down the notification and tap Hide teddy 🙈, or use the Teddy tile in Quick Settings.

🔐 Is it safe?

MyBubble has no internet access and no ads. Your number is stored only on your phone. The Accessibility permission is used only to type into the text box you selected.
